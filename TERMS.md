## What the service does

Udhaar helps people record physical items they lend or borrow and request
acknowledgment of a handover and a return. It does not transfer money, guarantee
repayment or return, verify item ownership, resolve disputes, or provide legal or
financial advice. A recorded action is a record of an app or link interaction;
its significance in a dispute depends on the facts and applicable law.

## Sharing and acknowledgment

Off the Record entries are private from other ordinary users. On the Record
requests go to the selected recipient's app account or to an email guest link.
Anyone holding a guest link can view and act on that loan without signing in;
the link does not independently verify identity. Links and account-bound requests
expire after 30 days. Replacing a request revokes its predecessor and requires
fresh handover acknowledgment.

Sending a request does not mean it was delivered or accepted. Handover
acknowledgment and return acknowledgment are separate. For shared returns, the
lender must confirm receipt; a borrower reporting a return alone leaves it
pending. Do not rely solely on Udhaar for important agreements or proof of a debt.
